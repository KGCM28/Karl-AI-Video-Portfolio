About Me

I'm Karl — an AI video editor and generator exploring the frontier of generative video.

My journey started with traditional video editing, but when generative AI video emerged, I dove in headfirst. I'm early in my career, but I move fast — constantly experimenting with new models, building custom ComfyUI workflows, and staying current with the rapid evolution of AI video tools.

Based in San Francisco, working globally. Always open to collaborating with creators, agencies, and brands pushing the boundaries of AI video.
