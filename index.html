About Me

Hi, I'm Karl. I've spent the last 3 years at the intersection of traditional video production and generative AI.

My journey started in commercial video editing — cutting docs, commercials, and brand films. When generative video hit, I didn't see a threat. I saw a new creative palette.

Today, I help brands and creators produce video content that would've been impossible (or impossibly expensive) just a year ago. Whether it's generating entirely new worlds with Runway, building custom ComfyUI pipelines for consistent characters, or editing AI footage into coherent narratives — I bridge the gap between what AI can do and what your audience needs to see.

Based in San Francisco, working globally.
